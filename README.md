## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **GRAIN ROT**. This trainer enhances expeditions by protecting fragile wooden vessels, improving movement and salvage efficiency, preserving recovered loot, and supporting safer progression through the Deep Layers.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, faster Outpost progression, safer resource runs, or additional control while exploring the Deep Layers.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Vessel Durability** — Prevents the active wooden vessel from breaking during an expedition.
- **Fire Protection** — Protects the current vessel from catching fire.
- **Spark Survival** — Extends the Spark's survival window after a vessel breaks.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Allows extended movement and physical interactions without exhausting stamina.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts player movement speed for faster exploration or controlled navigation.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Resource Multiplier** — Increases the amount of resources gained from salvage and environmental interactions.
- **Loot Protection** — Preserves collected loot when the expedition ends unsuccessfully.
- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides a large gold balance for Outpost rebuilding and available upgrades.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Damage Protection** — Reduces or prevents damage from Corrupted vessels and hazardous encounters.
- **Enhanced Impact** — Improves the effect of physical attacks, dropkicks, and other impact-based actions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement options after entering an expedition and gaining control of a vessel. Use the speed adjustment carefully in tight corridors, around hazards, and during physics-based interactions. Unlimited stamina is useful for repeated movement and physical actions, but normal positioning remains important when avoiding Corrupted enemies.

### Tools & Inventory

Resource and gold options should be enabled only after the game has loaded the relevant expedition or Outpost screen. Collect or process at least one item before using quantity adjustments. Keep loot protection enabled when experimenting in the Deep Layers, since an expedition can normally lose its loot if every vessel breaks.

### World & Environment

GRAIN ROT sends crews through procedurally shifting underground ruins accessed by a cursed elevator. Explore gradually, salvage furniture and resources, and return to the extraction point before the entire crew is lost. Between runs, use recovered value to rebuild the Outpost, unlock interactive furniture, improve statistics, rescue survivors, and prepare for deeper layers.

### Combat & Weapons

Corrupted vessels react to sound, movement, and nearby activity. Damage protection is most effective when enabled before entering an encounter. Enhanced impact supports the game's physical combat, including dropkicks and environmental chaos. When a vessel breaks, continue as a Spark and search for another host before the Spark fades.
